THE GRIMOIRE - PORTABLE  (version {{VERSION}})
=========================================

A deck builder and collection tracker for Magic: The Gathering.

To start: double-click "The Grimoire.exe".
The app opens in its own window. Close the window and everything shuts down.

Requirements on the PC you plug into
  - Windows 10 or 11 (Microsoft Edge is built in; Google Chrome also works)
  - An internet connection for card search, images and prices (Scryfall)
  Nothing needs to be installed.

Where your data lives
  Your decks, collection, ledger, watchlist and price history are saved inside
  the "data" folder next to the .exe, so they travel with the drive.
  - Close the app window before unplugging the drive.
  - Keep the whole "The Grimoire" folder together.
  - To back up, use the app's Backup option, or copy the "data" folder.

Updating to a new version
  Replace "The Grimoire.exe" and the "app" folder with the new ones.
  Leave the "data" folder where it is; your saved data carries over.

Folder contents
  The Grimoire.exe     the launcher (the one button)
  app\                 the app itself
  data\                your saved data (created on first run)
  LICENSE.txt          license for the app's code (MIT)
