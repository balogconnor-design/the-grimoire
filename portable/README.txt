COMMANDER DECK - PORTABLE
=========================

To start: double-click "Commander Deck.exe".
The app opens in its own window. Close the window and everything shuts down.

Requirements on the PC you plug into
  - Windows 10 or 11 (Microsoft Edge is built in; Google Chrome also works)
  - An internet connection for card search, images and prices (Scryfall)
  Nothing needs to be installed.

Where your data lives
  Your decks and collection are saved inside the "data" folder next to the .exe,
  so they travel with the drive.
  - Close the app window before unplugging the drive.
  - Keep the whole "Commander Deck" folder together.
  - To back up, use the app's Backup option, or copy the "data" folder.

Folder contents
  Commander Deck.exe   the launcher (the one button)
  app\                 the app itself
  data\                your saved data (created on first run)
